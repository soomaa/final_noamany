Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Noamany Fitness Center app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Al-Nomany (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application also collects the information you provide when you register and use the service, such as your name, phone number, email address, and membership details. To provide gym and fitness features, the Application may store your subscription and payment records, attendance history, class bookings, wallet points, and body-composition (InBody) measurements.

Camera Permission

The Application requires access to your device's Camera (android.permission.CAMERA). The camera is used solely for the purpose of the application's core functionality, such as taking profile pictures or capturing documents. We do not record or share video/images in the background, and all media captured is handled securely.

Location Information

The Application may collect and process information about your actual location. We use various technologies to determine location, including IP address, GPS, and other sensors. This is used for features like geo-fenced attendance and check-ins.

Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to help the Service Provider improve the Application and their service. The Application does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect specified information.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider will retain your personal data and automatically collected information only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as long as your account is active. We will retain and use your information to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

Data Deletion Policy

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data that we have collected. You can request to delete your account and associated data directly within the Application (if supported), or you can contact the Service Provider to request data deletion. Upon receiving your verifiable request, we will delete or anonymize your personal information from our records within a reasonable timeframe, unless retaining the information is necessary for us to comply with a legal obligation.
